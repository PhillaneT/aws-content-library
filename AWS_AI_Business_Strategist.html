AWS Certified AI Business Strategist

The first AWS certification built to validate business judgment for AI, not technical implementation. Designed for product managers, sales professionals, operations leaders, consultants, and business analysts who evaluate, champion, or scale AI initiatives alongside technical teams. No coding or AWS implementation experience required.
